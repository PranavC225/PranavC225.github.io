I'm a Machine Learning / AI engineer. I completed an MSc in Statistics & Machine Learning at Linköping University in June 2026, and I build LLM and computer-vision systems that come with a way to tell whether they actually work.

**What I do:**
- **Generative AI & agentic systems:** multi-agent RAG routed through LangGraph state machines, vector search over Qdrant and ChromaDB, shipped as full-stack apps on cloud infrastructure.
- **Measuring what I build:** my [RAG ablation study](/portfolio/rag-ablation/) scores pipeline variants against a ground-truth QA set with BERTScore and ROUGE. It found that retrieving *more* context degraded every metric at the chunk size I was using, the opposite of the intuition I started with.
- **Computer vision:** YOLO-based detection and tracking, carried from R&D through to deployment.
- **Classical ML & deep learning:** interpretable models across the range, from regression through fine-tuned pretrained networks.

**Recent work:**
- **Master's thesis @ PostNord** *(Jan – Jul 2026)*. Reframed parcel dwell-time prediction as a survival-analysis problem across a network of ~6,000 parcel lockers and ~12M annual deliveries, benchmarking models and ranking features against a target of raising locker utilisation from 1.25× to 2.25×.
- **AI Intern @ TCS Sverige** *(Jul 2025 – Jun 2026)*. AI GTM Nordics: built four Copilot agents automating workflows across five departments, co-designed an AI maturity assessment framework spanning seven business domains, and deployed an AI-assisted SDLC framework at TCS Pace Studio.
